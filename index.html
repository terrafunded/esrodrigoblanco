01Story

From Guatemala to Texas land

Rodrigo Blanco is the founder and CEO of TerraFunded, a Dallas-based company that sells rural Texas land directly to buyers with owner financing. He built it together with his wife and co-founder, Luisa de Blanco.

  1. Origin

    Born in Guatemala

    Rodrigo is Guatemalan and builds his businesses in the United States. He works in English and Spanish, and still answers buyers personally in both.

  2. 2019

    Portafolio Diversificado

    Rodrigo and Luisa co-found Portafolio Diversificado, focused on residential real estate in the United States. It's where he starts developing the seller-financing model that later defines TerraFunded.

  3. 2020

    TerraFunded

    They found TerraFunded, a separate company that subdivides rural Texas land into parcels of about 10 acres and sells them directly to buyers with owner financing and a Warranty Deed.

  4. 2026

    350+ transactions, $15M+ in sales

    TerraFunded passes $15 million in cumulative land sales across Texas counties such as Lamar, Young, Titus, Cass, Clay and Coryell, as reported by The Title Report.

  5. Today

    Technology built in-house

    Rodrigo leads a bilingual team of more than a dozen people that builds its own software with AI, including TerraFunded GPS, which shows buyers their lot lines through the phone camera.

02Vision

How I think about this business

  1. 01

    Own it from day one.

    I don't sell with a contract for deed or rent-to-own. If you're paying for land, it should be in your name from the closing table, with title insurance, at an independent title company.

  2. 02

    Judge the person, not the paperwork.

    Many good buyers have the income and the discipline but don't fit a bank's file. Financing the land ourselves lets more people own it, under a fixed-rate note with no prepayment penalty.

  3. 03

    An old industry, new tools.

    Rural land is one of the least digital industries in America. We build our own software with AI, from finding land to letting a buyer stand on a lot and see its boundaries through a phone camera.

  4. 04

    Speak the buyer's language.

    Contracts, guides and support in English and Spanish, so every buyer understands exactly what they sign.

  5. 05

    It's not acres. It's where a family builds.

    This fall I watched a buyer raising his house with his own hands on land he bought from us. That's what this business is for.
